How good is the soil-moisture model, really? Random splits versus villages and taluks it has never seen.

Finding: Random-split scores are near perfect, but on unseen villages no model beats the mean baseline by a useful margin. Soil moisture in this dataset behaves like a coarse satellite value shared by whole villages, so the model cannot replace in-field sensing. The engine accepts probe readings and uses the model only as a ranked fallback.
